<p align="center">
  PDFRaj is a free online toolkit for working with PDFs — merge, split, compress, convert to
  and from Word, Excel, PowerPoint and images, OCR scanned documents, and edit, sign or
  protect files. Everything runs in your browser with no account and no watermark, and every
  uploaded file is permanently deleted an hour after it's processed. The tools are powered by
  our own conversion engine, built and tuned in-house.
</p>

## What you get

- **40+ free PDF tools** — merge, split, compress, convert, OCR, edit, sign, protect
- **No account** — open a tool and go, nothing to sign up for
- **No watermark** — your files come out clean
- **Files deleted 1 hour after processing** — nothing is kept
- **Built on our own conversion engine** — tuned in-house for speed and quality
- **Works in any browser** — nothing to install
